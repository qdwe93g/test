## How to use these prompts

Generate each of the 20 images independently. Save the original PNG files with the exact names below in `voca_video/public/days/DAY3_21-30_set3/incoming_images/`.

**Style B canonical prefix:** Original polished stylized 3D game-adventure illustration, playful cinematic composition, soft lighting, rich colors, appealing original characters,
**Word images:** 1024×1024, 1:1.
**Sentence images:** 1376×768, wide 16:9. Keep the lower 24% visually simple for captions.
**Mandatory prohibition:** No text, no letters, no words, no numbers, no subtitles, no logo, no watermark in the image.

**OUTPUT FRAMING CONSTRAINT (apply to every image): Deliver one full-bleed image only, filling the entire requested canvas edge to edge. Do not create a frame, border, rounded card, Polaroid, mat, poster, collage, split panel, UI, device screen, thumbnail, or empty outer margin. Keep every important subject and object fully visible inside the inner 12% safe area on all four sides; do not crop heads, faces, hands, wings, limbs, tools, or key objects at the canvas edge. Use a medium or wide shot, never an extreme close-up. The composition must look complete when placed directly edge to edge in a video.**

**SAFETY RECOVERY RULE (apply only if an image request is blocked): Do not stop or return an explanation. Retry with a calm, non-violent, child-friendly alternative that preserves the intended educational dictionary meaning. Remove or replace potentially sensitive elements such as hunting, fighting, threats, weapons, injury, fear, dangerous close-ups, identifiable people, brands, or named animals; prefer a neutral everyday object, peaceful environment, distant wildlife observation, or simple visual metaphor. Keep all other output constraints and the exact target aspect ratio.**
